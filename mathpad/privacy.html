1. Who we are

MathPad is an app for writing, presenting and recording maths solutions, made by Preps233 in Accra, Ghana ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information MathPad uses and why. It covers the MathPad apps for Android and Windows and this website.

Questions or complaints about privacy: johnshiburah@gmail.com.

2. What MathPad stores on your device

Everything you create in MathPad is saved only on the device you use it on:

  • questions, solution steps, comments, diagrams, tables and handwriting;
  • pictures and videos you add to questions (MathPad keeps a copy in its own folder);
  • voice recordings made while you present, and the videos MathPad makes from them;
  • your settings, such as theme, pen colour and the text of your video end card.

We do not receive any of this. MathPad has no MathPad account, no cloud storage of its own and no servers that collect your content. If you uninstall MathPad, this data is removed from the app (videos you saved to your gallery or another folder stay where you saved them).

3. Device permissions

  • Microphone: used only while you record a presentation. The recording is processed on your device to make the video.
  • Camera, photos and files: used only when you choose to add a picture or video, import questions, or save a video.
  • Internet: used only to connect to and upload to YouTube, Facebook or TikTok when you ask MathPad to.

4. YouTube and Google

MathPad uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your YouTube channel when you ask it to. Connecting YouTube is optional.

Permission you grantWhat MathPad does with it
youtube.upload (manage your YouTube videos: upload)Uploads the video you choose to your channel, with the title, description, tags and privacy setting you enter. MathPad does not change or delete your other videos.
youtube.readonly (view your YouTube account)Reads your channel name once, to show which channel is connected. Nothing else from your account is read.

Storage. The sign-in tokens Google gives MathPad, and your channel name, are stored encrypted on your device (Android Keystore on Android, Windows data protection on Windows). They are sent only to Google, to upload videos.

Use. MathPad does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, does not transfer it to anyone else, and no person reads it. MathPad's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google's policies. Using YouTube through MathPad is also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access. Tap Disconnect next to YouTube in MathPad (Home → Video & publishing settings → Publishing accounts). This revokes MathPad's access with Google and deletes the tokens from your device. You can also remove MathPad's access at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

5. Facebook

Connecting Facebook is optional. Facebook allows apps to post only to Pages you manage, not to personal profiles.

Permission you grantWhat MathPad does with it
pages_show_listLists the Pages you manage so you can choose which one to post to.
pages_read_engagementReads each Page's name and the Page access token needed to post. MathPad does not read your posts, comments, followers or insights.
pages_manage_postsPosts the video you choose to the Page you choose, with your title and description.

Your Page names and access tokens are stored encrypted on your device and are sent only to Facebook. To revoke access, tap Disconnect in MathPad, which removes MathPad's permissions on Facebook and deletes the tokens, or remove MathPad in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.

6. TikTok

Connecting TikTok is optional.

Permission you grantWhat MathPad does with it
user.info.basicReads your TikTok display name, to show which account is connected.
video.uploadSends the video you choose to your TikTok inbox, where you finish and post it in the TikTok app.
video.publishPosts the video you choose directly to your account, with the caption and privacy setting you choose, when you pick "Post directly".

Your TikTok tokens and display name are stored encrypted on your device and are sent only to TikTok. To revoke access, tap Disconnect in MathPad, which revokes MathPad's access with TikTok and deletes the tokens, or remove MathPad in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your information. The only information that leaves your device is what you ask MathPad to send: a video and its title, description and tags, uploaded to the platform you choose, together with the sign-in token that platform requires. Once a video is on YouTube, Facebook or TikTok, that platform's own privacy policy applies to it.

8. Cookies, ads and third-party content

The MathPad apps contain no advertising and no analytics or tracking code, and no third party serves content through them. MathPad does not use cookies or similar technology to collect information, in the apps or through this website. The secure storage MathPad uses on your device holds only your own sign-in tokens, as described above. This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's IP address to deliver them (see the Google Privacy Policy).

9. Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Connected accounts: tap Disconnect next to YouTube, Facebook or TikTok in MathPad. MathPad revokes its access with the platform straight away and deletes the stored sign-in from your device.
  • Your content: delete any question or video in the app, or uninstall MathPad to remove everything it stored.
  • On the platforms: you can also revoke access from Google's security settings, Facebook's Business integrations settings, or TikTok's app permissions.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you would like us to confirm this, or you have any request about your data, email johnshiburah@gmail.com and we will reply within 7 days.

10. Security

Sign-in tokens and app keys are kept in the operating system's secure storage. All connections to YouTube, Facebook and TikTok use HTTPS. MathPad signs in through your web browser, so it never sees your passwords.

11. Children

MathPad is made for teachers and learners and does not collect personal information from anyone. Connecting YouTube, Facebook or TikTok requires an account on that platform, and each platform sets its own minimum age. A teacher or parent should connect those accounts for younger students.

12. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and we will explain any important change in the app. Questions, requests or complaints: johnshiburah@gmail.com.
