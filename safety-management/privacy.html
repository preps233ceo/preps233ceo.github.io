Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Safety Management System desktop app (“the app”) handles, why, and the choices organisations and users have. The app is made by Preps233, Accra, Ghana (“we”). Contact us at johnshiburah@gmail.com.

In short: the app keeps its records on the computer where it is installed and, if the owner turns it on, in a backup folder in the owner's own Google Drive. Records are not sent to us. We do not sell data, show ads, or use anyone's data to train AI models.

1. Who is responsible for the data

Each organisation that installs the app decides who has an account and what is recorded. The organisation is the controller of the personal data in its installation. We provide the software only and do not receive the records. If you are an employee with a question about your data, please contact your organisation first; you can also contact us.

2. Information the app stores on the computer

3. Google user data

The owner of an installation may choose to connect a Google account so the app keeps a backup in Google Drive and can restore it on another computer. Sign-in happens on Google's own page in the web browser; the app never sees the Google password.

What the app accesses

  • Google Drive, files created by the app only (drive.file): the app creates a folder called “Safety Management System – Backup” and saves inside it a copy of the installation's records (a compressed database file, plus one dated copy per day for the last 30 days) and the photos and documents attached to records. When the owner restores a backup, the app reads these same files back. With this permission the app cannot see or open any other files in the account.
  • The account's email address (openid, email): shown in the app so the owner can see which account backups go to.

How it is used, stored and shared

  • Google user data is used only to provide the feature the owner turned on: backing up and restoring that installation's own records, and showing which account is connected.
  • The permission Google issues (a refresh token) is stored on that computer, encrypted with the operating system's own protection. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Google user data is not transferred to third parties, except as needed to provide the feature (for example, to Google itself), for security, or to comply with the law.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting

The owner can press Disconnect under File → Backup & sync at any time, which deletes the stored permission from the computer. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in Drive stay there until you delete them.

4. Information sent over the internet

Apart from the optional Google Drive backup and optional Paystack payments, the app does not send records anywhere. It does not include analytics or advertising code.

5. How long data is kept

The organisation decides how long records are kept and can delete them in the app. Daily backup copies older than 30 days are deleted from Drive automatically; the latest backup stays until the owner deletes it.

6. Security

PINs are stored only in hashed form; repeated wrong PINs are blocked for 15 minutes; each user sees only what their role allows; photos and documents are only shown to people allowed to see the record; and the Google permission is stored encrypted. The organisation is responsible for protecting the computer itself (Windows sign-in, disk encryption and physical access).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)), you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data. Because the data is held by your organisation, please send such requests to it; we will help organisations respond.

8. Children

The app is meant for workplaces and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
