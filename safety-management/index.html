Safety Management System is a Windows app for health, safety and environment teams. Report incidents and observations, run permits to work, inspections and audits, and keep ISCC traceability records – all on your own computer, backed up to your own Google Drive.

Ask for the app

What it does

Incidents & observationsReport incidents, near misses and unsafe acts with photos, follow them up and close them with corrective actions.
Permits to workHot work, work at height, confined space, electrical isolation and more, with step-by-step approvals and sign-off.
Inspections & auditsChecklists and audit templates that score themselves and raise corrective actions for every failed item.
People & assetsTraining matrix, sick log, vehicles, PPE and stores inventory, waste, energy and water, and a procedures library.
ISCC traceabilityIncoming, processing and outgoing records, mass balance, sustainability declarations and a GHG calculator.
Fast and offlineEverything runs on the computer, so every screen opens at once, with or without internet.

How it uses Google

The owner of each installation can connect a Google account of their choice so the app keeps a backup of its records, photos and documents in that account's Google Drive. On a new computer, signing in with the same Google account restores everything. The app asks only to create and manage the files it makes itself (in a folder called “Safety Management System – Backup”) and to see the account's email address. It cannot see any other files in the Drive. Read the privacy policy for details.

Who it is for

Companies, factories, mills, estates and contractors that need to keep safety, environmental and ISCC records. Each installation is set up by an owner, who creates accounts for staff.
